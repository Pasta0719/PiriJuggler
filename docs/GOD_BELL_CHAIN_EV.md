# GOD bell-chain V-stock research note

Status: rejected for production design.

We evaluated a GG-internal bell-streak V-stock mechanic because it was a liked feature from Gaisen.

Under the high-pure-increase direction chosen for Piri GOD, the required bell frequency makes a short guaranteed streak reward consume too much expected value. The mechanic is therefore not part of the production GOD design.

The exact bell-chain model remains in the repository only as a research/analysis utility. It must not be wired into the production GOD engine unless the design decision is explicitly revisited later.
